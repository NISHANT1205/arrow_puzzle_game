Arrow Puzzle Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

About this policy

This policy applies to Arrow Puzzle (Android package com.nisharc.arrowpuzzle).

Data collection and sharing

Arrow Puzzle does not collect or transmit personal information, device identifiers, usage analytics or location data. The game does not include advertising, tracking or an account system. Gameplay works offline.

Progress and settings

Your unlocked level, completion count, sound, vibration and theme preferences are saved on your device. These values are used only to restore your progress and settings. They remain until you reset progress, clear the app data or uninstall the game. Android device backups may include app preferences according to your device backup settings; Arrow Puzzle does not operate its own backup service.

Your choices

Use Settings > Reset progress to reset your unlocked level and completion count. Sound, vibration and theme can be changed individually in Settings. To remove all stored preferences, clear the app data in Android settings. Device backups are managed through your device backup settings.

Children and personal information

The game does not ask players for personal information and does not collect personal information from children or other players.

Contact and changes

For questions about this policy, use the developer contact email shown on the Arrow Puzzle Google Play listing. If you contact the developer by email, your email address and message are used to respond to your request. If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated.
